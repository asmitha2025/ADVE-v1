# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

**THIS MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of execution by and between:

1. **ADVE TECHNOLOGIES** ("Discloser"), having its principal place of business at Bengaluru, Karnataka, India; and
2. **[COMPANY NAME]** ("Recipient"), having its principal place of business at [Company Address].

---

### 1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning the evaluation, benchmarking, and integration of the **Anchor-Delta Video Embedding (ADVE) System** software and neural video encoding optimization technology ("Purpose").

### 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:
- Source code, software architectures, algorithms, patent specifications, benchmark results, compatibility reports, trade secrets, and API documentation related to ADVE.
- Technical, financial, customer, and business operational information disclosed by either party.

### 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECIPIENT
The Recipient agrees to:
- Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature, but not less than reasonable care.
- Restrict disclosure of Confidential Information solely to employees, contractors, and legal advisors who have a strict need-to-know for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations.
- Not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or copy any software binaries or Docker images provided for evaluation.

### 4. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of **two (2) years** from the date of execution. Obligations of confidentiality with respect to trade secrets and proprietary algorithms shall survive termination indefinitely.

### 5. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
