# 🌐 ADVE Target Industry Catalog & Market Use-Cases

## Executive Overview
**Anchor-Delta Video Embedding (ADVE v3.1)** is a horizontal Video AI infrastructure middleware. Any industry that processes, indexes, or searches live or archived video feeds can deploy ADVE to reduce GPU cloud inference bills and storage costs by **60% to 85%**.

---

## 🏬 The 7 Core Target Industries

### 1. 🚦 Smart City & Traffic Management Systems
- **Use-Case**: Live traffic monitoring, speed violation search, vehicle classification, highway accident detection.
- **Why ADVE**: Reduces 24/7 GPU server costs for thousands of municipal CCTV streams by 60%.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: Tata Elxsi, L&T Smart World, Reliance Jio Infra, Honeywell Building Solutions.

### 2. 🛍️ Retail, Supermarkets & Shopping Malls
- **Use-Case**: Aisle shopper tracking, loss prevention/theft search, store foot-traffic analytics.
- **Why ADVE**: Delivers over 75% GPU savings on quiet store feeds, packing 15–18 camera streams per GPU.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: Reliance Retail, Tata Trent, DMart, Shoppers Stop, Amazon Physical Stores.

### 3. 🏦 Banking & Financial High-Security Facilities
- **Use-Case**: Bank vault monitoring, ATM booth security, cash-in-transit van tracking, perimeter intrusion alert.
- **Why ADVE**: Bank vaults are static 98% of the time, yielding over 80% GPU savings with 99.7% vector accuracy.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, State Bank of India, CMS Info Systems, Brink's.

### 4. 🏭 Industrial Manufacturing & Warehouses
- **Use-Case**: Factory floor safety compliance (helmet/vest detection), warehouse inventory tracking, conveyor belt analytics.
- **Why ADVE**: Eliminates redundant GPU processing on static manufacturing lines while capturing safety events.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: Tata Steel, Reliance Industries, Mahindra Logistics, Flipkart, Amazon Logistics.

### 5. 🚆 Airports, Seaports & Transportation Hubs
- **Use-Case**: Airport terminal crowd monitoring, train station platform safety, port container tracking.
- **Why ADVE**: Allows high-density camera deployments (2.4x camera capacity per server).
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: Indian Railways, GMR Airports, Adani Ports & Logistics, Delhi Metro.

### 6. 📹 CCTV Hardware & NVR/DVR System Manufacturers
- **Use-Case**: Embedding ADVE SDK into Network Video Recorders (NVRs) and Security Operations Center (SOC) software.
- **Why ADVE**: Allows camera manufacturers to market "AI Video Search" on low-cost edge chips.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: Hikvision India, Dahua Technology, CP PLUS, Prama, Honeywell Security.

### 7. 🎬 OTT Media, Sports & Broadcast Archives
- **Use-Case**: Natural language video scene search ("find goal at 42nd minute"), news media archival retrieval.
- **Why ADVE**: Indexes 10,000+ hours of archived video at 500+ video-hours per day per GPU.
- **Target Enterprise Clients**: JioCinema, Disney+ Hotstar, Sony LIV, Zee Archives.
